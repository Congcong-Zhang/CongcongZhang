About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in astrophysics at Xiangtan University.

I obtained my Ph.D. in Astrophysics from the University of Science and Technology of China.

My research focuses on the interstellar medium, astrochemistry and infrared astronomy, especially the evolution of PAHs and carbonaceous molecules.

Research Interests

JWST Infrared Astronomy

Interstellar Chemistry

Computational Astrochemistry

DFT calculations, Gaussian simulations and kinetic modeling.

Star Formation & PDRs

Understanding photochemical evolution in interstellar environments.
